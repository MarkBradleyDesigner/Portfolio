Various Projects

Singapore Airlines

KrisFlyer Dining. Earn and burn miles at Singapore's best restaurants.

Singapore Airlines wanted to grow its KrisFlyer frequent flyer programme beyond the flight, into members' everyday lives. The idea was to bring restaurant discovery and booking right into the rewards ecosystem

I led the product team that designed and built the app. Members sign in with their existing KrisFlyer login, search and book restaurants across Singapore, earn miles every time they dine, and redeem points for discounts at their favourite places. The app is geo-fenced to Singapore, so every recommendation is local and relevant, and it turns a good meal into another reason to stay loyal to the airline.

Qatar Olympic Museum

Brand Guidelines. One design language across a roster of agencies.

The 3-2-1 Qatar Olympic and Sports Museum brought together multiple agencies, each responsible for a different part of the visitor experience. Without one clear set of rules, the museum risked looking and feeling different from one gallery to the next.

I developed the design guidelines that kept every agency on the roster working from the same playbook. They covered environmental graphics, digital and interactive displays, and all communication materials, so visitors get one consistent experience from the entrance to the final exhibit.

NBPA

Map My Money. Putting young players in control of their finances.

Many young professional players earn more in a few seasons than most people do in decades, often before anyone has shown them how to manage it. The National Basketball Players Association wanted to change that, especially for the young Black players most exposed to bad advice and short careers.

Map My Money gives players one clear view of their whole financial life. It brings income, expenses, investments and endorsement deals together in a simple dashboard with real-time insights, so they can see where their money is going and make confident decisions. I led the design, turning complex financial data into an experience that feels approachable and puts players in control.

Darwinex

Onboarding Redesign. A clearer path from sign-up to first trade.

Darwinex is an online trading platform built for traders who want to grow and be backed by investors. Its product was powerful, but new users had to work hard to get from sign-up to their first trade, and the brand felt different at each step along the way.

We redesigned the user flows from onboarding through to the "Get Started" page, cutting friction so new users understand what to do next and reach their first trade with confidence. Alongside the flows, we evolved the brand's core elements and built them into every step of the journey and the wider design system, so the product looks and feels like one brand from first click to first trade.

Oysho

Training App. Bringing fitness into a fashion brand's digital world.

Oysho, the Inditex brand known for activewear, wanted to go beyond selling the clothes and become part of how its customers train. The challenge was to bring a training app into its online ecosystem without losing the feel of the brand.

We explored how the app could be woven into Oysho's existing online experience, offering workout sessions, challenges and programmes across fitness, yoga, Pilates and running, designed for every level. The result was a concept where shopping and training sit side by side, giving customers a reason to come back every day, not just when they need new kit.

Evo Bank

Reinventing the High Street Branch. From bank branch to financial planning centre.

EVO Bank set out to disrupt the Spanish market by rethinking what a high street branch is for. Instead of a place for transactions, select urban branches became annual financial planning centres offering private-bank-level service and comfort to everyday customers.

We designed the whole experience around that idea: choreographed planning meetings, personalised financial plans, targeted digital messages, and simple infographic product descriptions that made banking easy to understand. The strategy built on the bank's existing customers, turning great experiences into word-of-mouth referrals.

LearnWorlds

The Best of Both Worlds. Launching our first stand at Learning Technologies London.

For its first ever exhibition, LearnWorlds chose Learning Technologies in London, the world's leading eLearning expo for business. We had one stand to introduce the brand to a room full of competitors and buyers, and it had to explain the platform at a glance.

We created the positioning "The Best of Both Worlds" to show LearnWorlds' two big strengths: powerful content authoring tools and a robust LMS, together in one platform. That idea drove everything, from the stand design and graphics to the messaging and supporting materials, so visitors understood what made LearnWorlds different within seconds of walking past.

Samsung

In-Store Appointments. Connecting personal bookings to in-store displays.

Samsung wanted to show how personal appointments could become part of the in-store experience. The goal was to make a booked visit feel personal from the moment the customer walks through the door.

I created an app that generates customer appointments and talks to the CMS behind the store's digital screens, so each appointment's details appear on the displays in the retail space. It turns standard signage into a personal welcome and shows how digital and physical retail can work as one connected experience.

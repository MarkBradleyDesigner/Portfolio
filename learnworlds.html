LearnWorlds

Brand system for a scaling SaaS

LearnWorlds is an AI-powered learning management system for training providers, course creators, and education networks. It lets them create, sell, and deliver online training from one platform — interactive video, assessments, communities, white-label academies, and built-in commerce. Founded by learning-science PhDs, it now serves over 14,000 learning businesses.

Process

With guidelines and a design system in place, we turned to process. Every task starts in Slack — marketing or any teammate pings me, we align on scope and timeframe. The job lands in Asana with briefs, timelines, and assignees. From there it moves to Figma then WordPress: front-end devs handle complex pages, while marketers build simple templated ones themselves.

Different Audiences

Within the brand guidelines, three distinct visual executions. The product look: the interface in situ, a desktop app highlighting features. The mobile look: a standalone native Android or iPhone app — learning on the go. The lines-of-business look: coaching, health, finance, employer training, nonprofit, and enterprise tell their unique stories.

Marketing Campaigns

Three examples we focused on: Black Friday, a two-day online webinar event called Worlds of Learning, and a positioning campaign on what learning management systems actually do differently. Before this, we didn't really do 360 campaigns. I helped implement that thinking across paid, earned, and organic media, landing pages, PR, video, and community posting. All three were tremendously successful in click-to-trial, which we measured.

Lead Marketing

Case studies and opinion pieces — thought leadership in a particular area that clients and trial users could read, digest, and apply to their schools. Before I joined, the quality was poor, both content-wise and visually. I brought in copywriters: properly qualified educators who were also strong writers. Production values and output quality increased dramatically.

Where we stand today

LearnWorlds now serves 14,000 customers globally. Those customers generate over one billion dollars in revenue through their schools on the platform, reaching 35 million learners.

Mark Bradley

Creative Director for B2B SaaS, fintech and brands that need to scale.

I build brands that work as hard as the business behind them. For more than 20+ years I've led creative teams across Europe, North America and Asia, turning strategy into brand systems, campaigns and memorial experiences.

I'm currently Creative Director at LearnWorlds, a B2B edtech SaaS company with about 200 staff. I report to the CEO and lead the creative team. In that time the company has grown its ARR by 650%. I own the brand system, paid and owned campaigns, events and the website, and I've built AI-powered brand tools that let non-designers produce on-brand work in minutes.

At Sngular, a technology and design consultancy with about 1,500 staff, I led brand and a design teams through a full rebrand that carried the company to its IPO. Earlier, at John Ryan, I spent two decades as Head of Design for North America and International markets, based in Madrid and Minneapolis. I designed brand and customer experiences for banks including BBVA, Chase and Bank of America, and that work was featured in The Economist.

I am a UK citizen based in Madrid, with the right to work in Spain, the EU and the UK. I work in English and Spanish.
